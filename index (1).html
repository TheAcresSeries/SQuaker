Book One · The Acres Series

Acres

📍 Gettysburg, Pennsylvania

On the acres of Gettysburg is Alicia Averys - a mind-traveler who can see the world through another’s eyes. From a pocketknife to a rookie card, Alicia can travel through time to witness her family’s past and the events of the American Civil war.

Alicia’s smile, a declared 10, fades as her final day of the school year is interrupted by vandals who had too much fun redecorating the school. While her classmates gossip about who did what and why, Alicia investigates the crime by traveling into the minds of the vandals. Can she figure out the vandals’ identities? Is it safe to tell anyone that she can go back in time to investigate crimes?

Book Two · The Acres Series

Scar

📍 Savannah, Georgia

Southern comfort and hospitality will have to wait as Alicia Averys and her cousin Daphne investigate mysteries in Savannah, Georgia - one of America’s most haunted cities.

On the edge of the water, just past Bay Street, is the Riverfront Exchange. A building where people can shop, wander and allegedly dine with the deceased crew of the shipwrecked Three-Fourteen. With Alicia’s mind-traveling ability, the two ladies will investigate if the rumors are true and whether the sailors are friend or foe. In the course of the investigation, they will find an unexpected guest who calls Daphne’s new residence home sweet home.

Book Three · The Acres Series

Arc

📍 Gettysburg, Pennsylvania

With two weeks to the 4th of July, Alicia Averys returns to the acres of Gettysburg where her mind-traveling ability takes her through firefly fields and on a quest to explore a hidden secret on the acres.

While her family questions how a cavern lay under the acres without anyone knowing, Alicia follows the path of her two cousins, Abigail and Russell, who explored the cavern more than a hundred years in the past. Alicia can’t change the fate of her cousins, but she can learn why only one cousin walked out of the cavern alive.
