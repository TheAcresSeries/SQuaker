Book One · The Acres Series

Acres

📍 Gettysburg, Pennsylvania

On the acres of Gettysburg is Alicia Averys - a mind traveler who can see the world through the eyes of the deceased and those still living their best days. From a pocketknife to a Ty Cobb rookie card, Alicia can travel through her family tree to explore the American Civil War and more importantly, prepare a party for her favorite Uncle Ricky. 

Alicia's smile, a declared 10, fades as her final day of school is interrupted by vandals who had too much fun redecorating the school. She replays the night by riding in the mind of the culprits as her classmates' clamor about who did what and why?  Will anyone believe she solved the crime by mind traveling? Or will telling the truth result in her being charged by the police?

Book Two · The Acres Series

Scar

📍 Savannah, Georgia

Alicia leaves the school year behind in search of Southern Comfort and hospitality in the City of Savannah. Her quest is quickly put on hold as the haunting of the Riverfront Exchange requires her full attention and abilities to determine who or what haunts the patrons of the establishment. With help from her cousin, Daphne, who has her own supernatural ability, the two ladies will join forces to pursue ghosts and unexpectedly learn about a little Miss Daisy who calls Daphne's home her own.

Book Three · The Acres Series

Arc

📍 Gettysburg, Pennsylvania

Summer has returned to the acres of Gettysburg, Pennsylvania, and so has Daphne, who travels north from Savannah to join Alicia for the Fourth of July. Acting on clues gathered through her mind-travels, Alicia has pinpointed a cave entrance that holds a vast underground world of stalactites, a hidden stream, and walls lined floor to ceiling with crystals that seem to hold entire landscapes inside their faces. Deep in that glittering dark, Russell and Abigail have followed a thread of their own, venturing through the cave with a lantern, a pocketknife, and the stubborn courage that has always defined them. Meanwhile, back on the surface, Alicia watches the whole thing unfold from inside their minds, helpless to intervene but unable to look away.

About the Author

Quaker

SQ

Shane is the author of The Acres Series —

Shane grew up a county East of Gettysburg, Pennsylvania — the same location where the Acres takes place. Drawn to the way history of the city, Shane writes stories where the past isn't gone, just waiting to be found. Acres is the first book in a YA supernatural trilogy following Alicia Averys, continuing in Scar and Arc.
